# Deigo - The River's Fist

**Origin:** A small riverside community deep in the Brazilian Amazon.
**Fighting style:** Capoeira footwork, close-range grappling and powerful counterpunches.
**Tournament goal:** Protect his community's river and keep its future in local hands.

## Backstory

Deigo grew up where the river was the road. His family repaired boats and carried
supplies between settlements, and he learned early that strength meant being useful:
hauling an engine out of the mud, steadying a canoe in a storm, or carrying a neighbor
home when the water rose.

His mother, a capoeira instructor, gave that strength direction. She taught him to
listen to an opponent's rhythm instead of charging through it. Deigo became an
unlikely combination of patient footwork and overwhelming power, earning the name
"The River's Fist" at regional fighting exhibitions.

Then a development company began buying control of the docks his community relied
on. Promised jobs became expensive permits, and damaged waterways threatened the
boat routes that connected families to clinics and markets. Deigo helped his
neighbors organize, but repairing boats and raising money could only take them so far.

An invitation to an international fighting tournament offered another route. Its
prize could fund an independent community dock, safer boats and the legal fight to
protect access to the river. Deigo accepted, only to discover that the company behind
the dock scheme was also one of the tournament's sponsors.

Now every match is more than a contest of strength. He must win without becoming
the reckless bruiser his opponents expect, and make the audience see the people
behind the place they call "the jungle."

## Personality

Fierce in the ring and gentle outside it, Deigo repairs other fighters' broken gear
and calls home before every bout. His biggest weakness is impatience when someone
else is in danger. His mother's advice keeps him grounded: "A river does not win by
striking once. It keeps moving."

**Victory line:** "This strength isn't just mine."
