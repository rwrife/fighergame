# Ayame Kisaragi - The Violet Artisan

**Age:** 26.
**Origin:** Tokyo, Japan.
**Occupation:** Costume maker, cosplayer and stage-combat performer.
**Fighting style:** Kickboxing, evasive stage-combat footwork and jujutsu counters.
**Tournament goal:** Save her independent costume workshop and prove that its creators
deserve the spotlight.

## Backstory

Ayame Kisaragi learned to sew before she learned to fight. In her family's small
costume workshop, a broken zipper could derail an entire performance, and a clever
repair could rescue it. She grew up believing that heroes were made by patient hands,
not born with perfect armor.

At conventions, Ayame became known for handmade costumes and elaborate stage duels.
Her violet-armored persona was her own creation: part theatrical swordswoman, part
walking demonstration of everything her workshop could build. The saber was a blunt
performance prop, but the balance, discipline and years of martial-arts practice
behind each movement were real.
In tournament matches she fights unarmed, turning that timing and balance into
precise punches, sweeping kicks and close-range counters.

When a major entertainment company copied the workshop's original designs and offered
its struggling artists an exclusive contract, Ayame refused. Accepting meant surrendering
their work; refusing meant losing the commissions that kept the lights on.

Then the international fighting tournament announced televised open qualifiers.
Ayame saw a stage too large for the company to control. Prize money could keep the
workshop independent, while a memorable performance would put the artists' names
in front of the audience that had never seen them.

The other fighters mistake her elaborate outfit for a disguise. Ayame lets them.
Every reinforced seam, flexible armor panel and carefully weighted sleeve was built
to move with her. She has spent her life making imaginary heroes convincing.
Now she must decide what kind of hero she wants to be when the performance ends.

## Personality

Meticulous, quietly competitive and generous with beginners, Ayame can spot a loose
stitch across a crowded room. She treats every opponent seriously but takes criticism
of her craftsmanship far too personally. Her greatest challenge is learning that a
flawed performance can still be an honest one.

**Victory line:** "Not bad for handmade."

## Visual Identity

Long violet hair, side-swept bangs and a silver origami-crane hairpin. Lavender
kimono-inspired layers, plum segmented shoulder and hip armor, gold-trimmed belts,
split coat tails, black trousers and knee-high boots. Her promotional scene includes
a purple cosplay stage saber; tournament combat and the T-pose are strictly unarmed,
with empty hands and no props or weapons.
